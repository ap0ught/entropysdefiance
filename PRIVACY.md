# Privacy Policy

**App:** Entropy's Defiance
**Last updated:** 2026-06-22

Entropy's Defiance is a minimalist Android launcher and task manager. It is
designed to be private by default: the app is offline-first and contains **no
trackers, no analytics, and no advertising**.

## Data we collect

**None.** The developer does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal
or device data. The app has no backend server and sends no data about you
anywhere.

## Data the app stores on your device

All data stays on your device (or in storage locations you choose):

- **Tasks** are stored as a plain JSON file on your device, in a folder you
  select. This file never leaves your device unless *you* choose to sync it
  yourself (for example, using a tool like Syncthing). Any such sync is entirely
  under your control and does not involve the developer.
- **App settings** (appearance, gestures, calendar selection, etc.) are stored
  locally on your device.

You can delete this data at any time by deleting the task file and/or
uninstalling the app.

## Permissions

The app requests only what it needs to function, and the data accessed never
leaves your device:

- **Query all packages** — to list your installed apps in the app drawer.
- **Read calendar** — only to display events in the optional calendar widget and
  to calculate free time. Calendar data is read on-device and is never
  transmitted or stored by the developer. You may deny this permission.
- **Set wallpaper** — used by the launcher to restore your wallpaper.
- **Bind device admin** — only requested if you opt in to Zen Mode's
  "Do Not Disturb" integration, and used solely for that purpose.

## Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.
